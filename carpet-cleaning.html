Professional Carpet Steam Cleaning

Deep clean your carpets with our eco-friendly hot water extraction method

Ireland's Trusted Carpet Cleaning Experts

Carpet steam cleaning, also known as hot water extraction, is widely recognised as one of the most effective methods for deep cleaning carpets. Our professional service uses high-temperature steam to penetrate deep into carpet fibres, removing dirt, bacteria, and allergens that standard vacuuming leaves behind.

At Go Green Steam Clean, we use advanced Airflex Storm technology to ensure a thorough, hygienic clean that's gentle on fibres and safe for your home. Our eco-friendly approach means no harsh chemicals - just powerful steam that leaves your carpets fresh, clean, and revitalised.

Our 5-Step Carpet Cleaning Process

We've perfected our cleaning process over 10+ years to deliver exceptional results every time:

  • Inspection: We assess your carpets and identify stains, high-traffic areas, and problem spots.
  • Pre-treatment: Eco-friendly solutions are applied to break down dirt and stubborn stains.
  • Deep Steam Clean: Hot water extraction using our advanced Airflex Storm technology.
  • Sanitisation: We apply sanitiser to eliminate bacteria, dust mites, and allergens.
  • Speed Drying: Rapid drying techniques minimise disruption - carpets dry within hours.

Every clean includes: Stain treatment, deodoriser, and sanitiser at no extra cost.

Professional Water Tank Cleaning

Ensure clean, safe water for your home or business with our expert cleaning service

Attic Water Tank Cleaning & Disinfection

Most homes in the country have attic water tanks, but not many people know that they should be cleaned regularly. Over time, water tanks build up bacteria, mold, algae, and viruses - not what you want to be showering and brushing your teeth with!

Domestic and commercial water tank cleaning is essential for water hygiene and legionella control. Sediment, silt, and rust can accumulate, leading to a significant deterioration of water quality and posing serious health risks if not maintained properly.

Why Clean Your Water Tank?

What could be lurking in your water tank

Bacteria & Viruses

Stagnant water becomes a breeding ground for harmful bacteria and viruses that can affect your health.

Mold & Algae

Dark, damp conditions in water tanks create ideal environments for mold and algae growth.

Sediment & Rust

Over time, sediment, silt, and rust accumulate at the bottom of your tank, contaminating your water supply.

Our 3-Step Cleaning Process

We follow a thorough cleaning process to ensure your water tank is completely clean and safe:

  • Step 1 - Drain: We drain the contaminated water from the tank completely.
  • Step 2 - Clean & Disinfect: We clean, disinfect, and chlorinate the inside of the tank to eliminate all bacteria, mold, and contaminants.
  • Step 3 - Refill & Treat: We refill the tank and add a decontamination solution to ensure ongoing water safety.

Recommendation: We recommend getting your water tanks cleaned at least once a year to maintain water quality and hygiene.

Benefits of Professional Water Tank Cleaning

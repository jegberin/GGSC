Trusted Steam Cleaning Across Offaly

Offaly neighbours our home county, so we’re in and out of it almost every week. Between the busy market towns — Tullamore, Birr, Edenderry, Clara — and the scattered rural homes out along the Slieve Blooms and the bog roads, there’s always work to be done. We travel with enough equipment to handle big country-home jobs in one visit.

Typical Offaly jobs: whole-house carpet cleans in Tullamore and Edenderry, sofa and upholstery cleans in three-bed semis, mattress sanitising for allergy sufferers, oriental and wool rug restoration for period homes in Birr, and commercial carpet cleans for cafes, shops and small offices across the county.

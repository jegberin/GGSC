Our Story

Go Green Steam Clean is a family-owned and operated business based in Portlaoise, County Laois. For over a decade, we've been providing professional steam cleaning services to homes and businesses across the Midlands.

What started as a passion for helping people maintain cleaner, healthier homes has grown into a trusted local business serving thousands of satisfied customers across Laois, Kildare, Carlow, Kilkenny, Tipperary, Offaly, and Westmeath.

We take immense pride in our work and treat every home as if it were our own. Our commitment to excellence, punctuality, and customer satisfaction has earned us a reputation as one of the most reliable steam cleaning services in the region.

The Airflex Storm Advantage

We invested in Airflex Storm — one of the most powerful hot-water extraction systems on the market — because the equipment makes a visible difference to the clean you receive.

  • Higher heat at the wand: steam hits carpet and upholstery fibres hot enough to break down grease, oils and the sticky residues that hold dirt in place.
  • Industrial-grade vacuum lift: pulls out up to 95% of the moisture in a single pass, so surfaces are typically touch-dry in 2–4 hours instead of 24–48.
  • Separate solution and recovery tanks: dirty water never re-contaminates clean areas — something cheaper rental machines can’t guarantee.
  • Eco-safe detergents: non-toxic, low-residue cleaning solutions that are safe for children, pets and sensitive skin.

What it means for you: a deeper clean that lasts longer, less disruption to your day, and none of the crunchy, chemical-smelling residue left behind by DIY or budget services.

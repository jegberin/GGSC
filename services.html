Water Tank Cleaning
  • Most homes in the country have attic water tanks. But not many people know that it should be cleaned regularly as it builds with bacteria, mold, algae and viruses. Not what you want to be showering and brushing your teeth with!
  • Domestic and commercial water tank cleaning is essential for water hygiene and legionella control. It is likely that over time your tank will develop sediment, silt and rust.
  • This can lead to a significant deterioration of water quality and if not maintained properly could pose serious health risks.
  • Regular cleaning and disinfecting of water tanks is essential to ensure the water stays clean and bacteria free. It is recommended to get the water tanks cleaned at least once a year.
  • For businesses, neglecting the cleaning and disinfecting of water tanks can lead to non-compliance with Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005.
  • Here is our cleaning process:
  • Step 1: Drain the contaminated water from the tank
  • Step 2: Clean, disinfect and chlorinate the inside of the tank
  • Step 3: Refill the tank and add a decontamination solution

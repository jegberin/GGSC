Better Sleep Starts with a Clean Mattress

We spend roughly a third of our lives on a mattress — and in that time it quietly collects dead skin, sweat, dust mites, bacteria and allergens. Changing the sheets doesn’t touch any of that. Only a proper deep clean does.

Our hot-steam process reaches temperatures well above 55°C at the mattress surface, the threshold at which dust mites die and the allergen proteins in their waste break down. That’s the single most effective step you can take for allergy and asthma sufferers. No harsh chemicals, no soaked mattress — typically dry enough to sleep on the same night.

Why Clean Your Mattress?

What's hiding in your mattress might surprise you

Dust Mites

The average mattress can contain millions of dust mites that feed on dead skin cells and can trigger allergies.

Bacteria

Sweat, body oils, and moisture create an ideal breeding ground for bacteria in your mattress.

Allergens

Pet dander, pollen, and other allergens accumulate in mattress fibres, affecting your sleep quality.

Benefits of Professional Mattress Cleaning

  • Eliminates dust mites and their waste products
  • Removes bacteria, fungi, and mould spores
  • Reduces allergy and asthma triggers
  • Removes stains, odours, and perspiration
  • Extends the lifespan of your mattress
  • Improves overall sleep quality
  • Safe for children and allergy sufferers
  • Quick drying time - use your bed the same day
